# Horizontal Cable Row

## Overview
The horizontal cable row is a compound pulling exercise performed on a cable machine that targets the entire middle back — rhomboids, middle trapezius, and lats. Unlike the barbell row which requires significant lower back involvement for stabilization, the seated cable row is performed in a supported position that isolates the back muscles more directly. The cable machine provides constant tension throughout the entire range of motion making it one of the best exercises for back thickness and the mind-muscle connection.

## Muscles Worked

- **Primary:** Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius, Latissimus Dorsi
- **Secondary:** Rear Deltoids, Biceps Brachii
- **Stabilizers:** Core, Erector Spinae (lightly)

## Equipment
- Cable machine with low pulley
- V-bar (close grip), wide bar, or rope attachment
- Seated cable row station or bench

## How To Perform

### Setup
1. Sit at the cable row station with your feet flat on the foot plate
2. Keep a slight bend in the knees — do not lock them out
3. Grip the attachment with both hands
4. Sit tall with a neutral spine — do not round the lower back
5. Start with arms fully extended — feel the stretch across the mid back

### The Movement
1. Take a breath and brace your core
2. Initiate the pull by retracting your shoulder blades — squeeze them together first
3. Drive your elbows back past your torso
4. Pull the handle into your lower abdomen or upper waist
5. Squeeze the entire mid back hard at the peak contraction for 1 second
6. Slowly return to the starting position — take 2-3 seconds
7. Let the shoulder blades protract fully at the front — feel the stretch
8. Exhale at the peak and repeat

## Rep and Set Recommendations

| Goal | Sets | Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | 3-4 | 6-8 | 90-120 secs |
| Hypertrophy (muscle growth) | 3-4 | 10-12 | 60-90 secs |
| Pump / Finisher | 2-3 | 12-15 | 45-60 secs |

## Progressive Overload
- Increase weight by the smallest available increment when you can complete all reps with full range of motion and a strong squeeze
- Focus on the stretch at the front and the squeeze at the back — quality over quantity
- Drop sets work very well on this exercise for finishing off the back

## Key Cues
- **"Shoulder blades first"** — initiate every rep by retracting the scapula before bending the elbows
- **"Sit tall throughout"** — never round the lower back or lean excessively to generate momentum
- **"Elbows past the body"** — drive the elbows behind the torso for maximum mid back activation
- **"Feel the stretch at the front"** — fully protract the shoulder blades at the start of every rep
- **"Squeeze and hold"** — pausing at peak contraction builds a stronger mind-muscle connection with the mid back

## Common Mistakes

- **Leaning back and rowing with momentum** — turns the exercise into a full body swing and removes back isolation. Fix: keep the torso upright and still throughout every rep
- **Not retracting the shoulder blades** — reduces rhomboid and trap activation. Fix: consciously initiate every rep with a scapular retraction
- **Partial range of motion** — not reaching full stretch at the front limits back development. Fix: let the arms extend fully and feel the shoulder blades spread apart at the front
- **Elbows flaring outward** — shifts load to the rear delts and away from the mid back. Fix: keep elbows traveling back close to the body
- **Jerking the weight** — uses momentum instead of muscle. Fix: use a controlled 2-second pull and 3-second return

## Horizontal Cable Row vs Barbell Row

| | Horizontal Cable Row | Barbell Row |
|---|---|---|
| Lower back stress | Low — supported position | High — unsupported hinge |
| Constant tension | Yes — cable provides it | No — tension varies |
| Mid back isolation | Higher | Lower |
| Weight potential | Moderate | Heavy |
| Back friendly | Very | Moderately |
| Best for | Isolation and hypertrophy | Strength and mass |
| Use both? | Yes — they complement each other |

## Attachment Variations and Their Effects

| Attachment | Grip | Primary Target |
|---|---|---|
| V-bar (close grip) | Neutral — palms facing each other | Lower lats and mid back |
| Wide bar — overhand | Palms facing down | Upper back and rear delts |
| Wide bar — underhand | Palms facing up | Lower lats and biceps |
| Rope | Neutral, pull apart at end | Maximum mid back squeeze |
| Single handle | One arm at a time | Imbalance correction |

## Where It Fits In A Back Day

```
Warm Up:
1. Dead hangs and band pull aparts        (activation — do this first)

Working Sets:
2. Barbell Deadlift                       (total body compound — do this second)
3. Pull Ups                               (vertical pull compound — do this third)
4. Barbell Row                            (horizontal pull compound — do this fourth)
5. Lat Pulldown                           (vertical pull volume — do this fifth)
6. Horizontal Cable Row                   (isolation finisher — do this last)
```

The horizontal cable row is the perfect back day finisher. By this point the entire back is already fatigued from heavy compound work. The cable row allows you to isolate the mid back with constant tension and squeeze out every last bit of volume with controlled, focused reps.

## The Stretch and Squeeze Protocol
For maximum hypertrophy on the cable row use this technique:
1. At the front — fully extend the arms and let the shoulder blades spread apart completely
2. Hold the stretch for 1 second — feel the mid back fully lengthened
3. Pull back with a 2-second concentric
4. At the back — squeeze the shoulder blades together as hard as possible
5. Hold the contraction for 1-2 seconds
6. Return slowly over 3 seconds

This stretch and squeeze protocol maximizes time under tension and builds a stronger mind-muscle connection than simply moving the weight back and forth.

## Safety Considerations
- Never round the lower back even though it is a supported exercise
- If you feel lower back discomfort, check that you are sitting tall and not leaning excessively
- Ensure the cable is at the correct low pulley position — a high pulley changes the muscle emphasis significantly
- Warm up with lighter sets before going to working weight

## Variations

| Variation | How | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Single arm cable row | One handle, one arm at a time | Fixes imbalances, greater rotation |
| High cable row | Pulley at upper chest height | More upper back and rear delt |
| Rope cable row | Rope attachment, pull apart at end | Maximum contraction at the back |
| Chest supported row | Incline bench in front of cable | Completely removes lower back |
| Meadows row | Landmine setup, single arm | Unique angle for lat thickness |
